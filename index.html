About Reaver Corp

Forged in darkness. Built on fear.

Our Mission

To entertain our public and offer different experiences, but always with a touch of fear that lingers long after the screen goes dark.

Our Origin

In 2024, Uriel had an idea on creating an enterprise about the horror industry along with his friend Alberto. Together, they set out to leave a mark on the world of psychological terror.

What We Create

We offer psychological horror games — mostly plausible and life-happening — designed to immerse the player in a real situation, making fear feel inevitable and inescapable.
